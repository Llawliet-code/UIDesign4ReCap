BSIT · 2024 · Machine Learning
Smart Attendance Monitoring System Using Facial Recognition with Machine Learning for CTU Daanbantayan Campus
Reyes, Angelo M. · Santos, Maria C. · Dela Cruz, Jose R. — Adviser: Prof. Elena Villanueva
Abstract
This study developed and evaluated a smart attendance monitoring system utilizing convolutional neural network-based facial recognition to automate student tracking at CTU Daanbantayan Campus. The system integrates a real-time camera feed processed by a Python-OpenCV pipeline, achieving 94.3% accuracy across varied lighting conditions. Testing with 280 enrolled student samples demonstrated a 78% reduction in manual recording time compared to traditional roll-call methods.
Key Findings
The system achieved 94.3% recognition accuracy, 78% reduction in attendance recording time, and an overall System Usability Scale (SUS) score of 84.2 (Grade A). Faculty reported significantly higher satisfaction compared to manual methods.
