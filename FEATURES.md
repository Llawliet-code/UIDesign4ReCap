## 🎨 Visual Features

### Dark Mode 🌙
**What it does:** Changes colors to be easier on your eyes

**How to use:**
1. Click the moon icon (🌙) in the top menu
2. Colors change from light to dark
3. Click sun icon (☀️) to switch back

**Why it's cool:**
- Better for nighttime use
- Saves battery on phones
- Looks modern and sleek
- Your choice is remembered

**Why it's cool:**

### Smart Filters
**What it does:** Narrow down your search

**Available filters:**
- **Year:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021
- **Program:** BSIT, BSCS, BSED, BSAgriBusiness
- **Topic:** Machine Learning, IoT, Web Dev, Mobile Apps

**How to use:**

**Why it's cool:**
- Find exactly what you need
- See active filters clearly
- Easy to remove filters

## ♿ Accessibility Features

### Keyboard Navigation ⌨️
**What it does:** Use website without a mouse

**How to use:**
- **Tab:** Move forward
- **Shift+Tab:** Move backward
- **Arrow Keys:** Switch tabs
- **Enter:** Click things
- **Escape:** Close dialogs

**Why it's cool:**
- Faster for power users
- Works with screen readers
- Required for some users

---

### Screen Reader Support 📢

**Why it's cool:**
